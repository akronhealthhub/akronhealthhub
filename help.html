Help Guide

How to Use the Akron Health Hub:

Click into a Service Category Tour to view a map of organizations that provide services in that category.
Click on each hotspot on the map to access Augmented Reality images and information about the services provided at that location.
Once in an organization's hotspot, click and drag with your mouse (or swipe on mobile) to look around the 360° image of the location. Arrows have been added to label key details, like the entrance.
Click the to access the hotspot Menu. The Menu contains links to the organization's Services and Contact/Hours, as well as:
  • Hotline information
  • How to use the Akron Health Hub
  • A link to leave us feedback
Within the Menu's pages, use the icon to go back to the last page you were on.
Click the icon to center your view of the 360° image.
Click the icon to return to the map of services in the tour you are viewing.
